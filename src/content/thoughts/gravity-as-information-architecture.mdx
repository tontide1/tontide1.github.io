# The Problem with Traditional Navigation

Almost every personal developer portfolio on the internet follows an identical wireframe:
- A sticky top navigation bar with: `[About] [Blog] [Projects] [Contact]`.
- A generic hero section with a portrait, a title, and social badges.
- A paginated grid of cards.

This structure assumes that all knowledge is flat, sequential, and organized into arbitrary buckets. But an engineer's work does not grow in buckets. It grows in **orbits**.

## Gravity as Spatial Meaning

In **TÀI — A Personal Gravity System**, information is organized around a stable center of mass:

1. **Center of Mass (Identity)**: The engineer is the gravitational anchor. The system exists because of their personal curiosity, judgment, and perspective.
2. **Orbits as Context**: A domain is not a folder on disk; it is an orbital plane. Concrete projects revolve closer or farther depending on operational maturity; research hypotheses circle as exploratory satellites.
3. **Relationships as Gravitational Attraction**: When a research benchmark directly informs an engineered system (like *Hybrid Legal QA* informing *Legal-RAG*), their spatial proximity in the mental map reflects real intellectual gravity.

> Exploration may be novel; reading must remain conventional.

The 3D celestial universe is the **map** — the discovery layer. When you choose to read, the universe recedes, and standard typography and accessible HTML take over.

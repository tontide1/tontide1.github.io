# Overview

**Legal-RAG** is an automated retrieval-augmented generation engine engineered specifically for Vietnamese statutory legislation and legal decrees.

In legal domains, standard semantic vector search frequently fails because citations, specific article references (e.g. *Điều 32 khoản 1*), and formal decree numbers have little semantic variance in vector space, yet represent the critical distinction between correct jurisprudence and an invalid citation.

```text
User Query
   ↓
Query Expansion & Clause Parsing
   ├────────────────────────┐
   ↓                        ↓
Dense Semantic Vector    BM25 Sparse Inverted Index
(Qdrant / pgvector)      (Tuned k1=1.2, b=0.75)
   │                        │
   └───────────┬────────────┘
               ↓
    Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF)
               ↓
    Cross-Encoder Reranker
               ↓
    Final Augmented Context for LLM
```

## Architectural Highlights

### 1. Statutory Chunking Strategy
Instead of uniform character-length chunking (which slices through paragraphs arbitrarily), Legal-RAG parses the legal hierarchy natively:
- **Law / Decree (Luật / Nghị định)**
- **Chapter (Chương)**
- **Article (Điều)**
- **Clause (Khoản)**
- **Point (Điểm)**

Every chunk preserves its ancestral hierarchy metadata in document payloads.

### 2. Hybrid Retrieval with RRF
We execute simultaneous retrieval across two distinct indices:
- **Dense index**: Captures semantic intent and natural-language query semantics.
- **Sparse BM25 index**: Guarantees deterministic recall of article numbers, monetary fines, and statutory identifiers.

The candidate sets are combined using Reciprocal Rank Fusion:

```text
RRF_Score(d) = Σ [ 1 / (60 + rank_m(d)) ]
```

### 3. Cross-Encoder Reranking
Top-30 candidates from the fusion stage pass through a bilingual cross-encoder model trained on legal question-passage pairs to produce calibrated relevance scores before context injection.

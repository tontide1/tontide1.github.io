# Problem Statement

Standard embedding models optimize for semantic proximity rather than statutory lexical precision. In Vietnamese legal retrieval, queries frequently hinge on:
1. Exact law identification (e.g. *Luật Đất đai 2024* vs *Luật Đất đai 2013*).
2. Article numbers (*Điều 12* vs *Điều 21*).
3. Exact penalty numbers (e.g. *phạt tiền từ 2.000.000 đồng đến 4.000.000 đồng*).

Dense retrieval models map "phạt tiền từ 2.000.000" and "phạt tiền từ 5.000.000" to near-identical vectors because the semantic structure is synonymous, leading to legal hallucinations if context injection is flawed.

## Experimental Setup

- **Corpus**: 14,000 statutory documents from the National Legal Database.
- **Query Set**: 500 annotated legal queries (300 conversational, 200 citation-specific).
- **Baselines**:
  1. Dense Only (BAAI/bge-m3)
  2. Sparse Only (BM25, k1=1.2, b=0.75)
  3. Hybrid Fusion (BM25 + BGE-M3 with RRF k=60)
  4. Hybrid + Cross-Encoder Reranker

## Interim Results

| Method | Recall@5 (Citation) | Recall@5 (Semantic) | NDCG@10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dense Only | 58.2% | 84.1% | 0.69 |
| BM25 Only | 89.4% | 61.3% | 0.72 |
| **Hybrid + Rerank** | **94.8%** | **88.6%** | **0.86** |

The hybrid approach provides the reliability floor necessary for production statutory QA.
